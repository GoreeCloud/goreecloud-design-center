Accessibility

Visual refinement must survive fallback.

The interface keeps hierarchy, labels, focus, and critical meaning when motion, transparency, color, or space is constrained.

Reduced Motion

Nonessential travel collapses to short state changes and fades.

Reduced Transparency

Glazed chrome becomes solid while hierarchy and semantics remain intact.

Forced Colors

System colors and visible boundaries replace decorative depth.
