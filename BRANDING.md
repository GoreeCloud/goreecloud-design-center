# GoreeCloud Design Center — Branding

**Canonical product name:** GoreeCloud Design Center  
**Short installed/display name:** Design Center  
**Lifecycle:** Development

The repository-local primary mark is `assets/design-center-mark.svg`. It uses a layered surface motif intended to communicate interface composition and design-system work while remaining distinct from the general GoreeCloud platform logo.

Design Center must remain recognizably GoreeCloud through current Stable Glaze UI semantics, geometry, hierarchy, typography, interaction behavior, and accessibility treatment.

Current repository artwork is a Development identity foundation. Full cross-platform identity acceptance, additional required derivatives, and the application umbrella identity remain pending governed acceptance work.
